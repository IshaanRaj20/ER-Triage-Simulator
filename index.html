How ER triage works

  1. A trained nurse checks for life threats first: airway, breathing, circulation, consciousness.
  2. Vital signs and pain are measured, not just described.
  3. Patients are ordered by urgency, not arrival time.
  4. Condition is re-checked while waiting, because it can change.
